Sources and interpretation

Raw counts are satellite observations of thermal anomalies. Repeated observations may refer to the same fire. MODIS and VIIRS have different resolutions and overpass times. Clouds, smoke, and gaps between overpasses can hide activity.

“No records” means the loaded file has records for that sensor and day, but none match this area. “Unknown” means the available data cannot establish an observation count. Neither label establishes fire absence.

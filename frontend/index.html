Data & methods

Raw counts are satellite observations of thermal anomalies. Repeated observations may refer to the same fire. MODIS and VIIRS have different resolutions and overpass times. Clouds, smoke, and gaps between overpasses can hide activity.

A zero means the available source reported no detections in the study area on that day. A question mark means the source is unavailable.

The calendar counts an H3 cell once for each UTC day with eligible activity. Monthly estimates convert standard-processing observations to a common Suomi-NPP scale. Recent provisional detections appear in the raw map and table. Calibration results
